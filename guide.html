📱 User Guide

Learn how to get the most out of Life Problem Solver

🚀 Getting Started

1

Install the App

Download the APK from our landing page and install it on your Android device.

Installation Note

Make sure to enable "Install from unknown sources" in your Android settings before installing the APK.

2

Complete Onboarding

When you first open the app, you'll go through a quick onboarding process:

  • Answer questions about your daily decision-making patterns
  • See how much time you might be losing on small decisions
  • Get personalized insights about your problem-solving habits

Pro Tip

Be honest with your answers - this helps the app provide better solutions tailored to your needs!

➕ Adding Problems

1

Tap the Add Button

On the main screen, tap the + button (Floating Action Button) to add a new problem.

2

Describe Your Problem

Fill in the problem details:

  • Title: A clear, concise title for your problem
  • Description: Detailed description of the issue
  • Category: Choose from Personal, Professional, Health, Financial, Relationships, Education, Technology, or Other
  • Priority: Set as Urgent, High, Medium, or Low
Edit Text Fields

Type or edit your problem title and description in the text fields

Voice Input

Use the microphone icon in the top bar to speak your problem instead of typing! This makes it faster and more natural.

Voice Input Icon

Tap the microphone icon to record your problem using voice

3

Save Your Problem

After filling in all the details, tap the "Submit Problem" button to save:

  • Make sure title and description are filled
  • Select appropriate category and priority
  • Tap the submit button at the bottom
Submit Button

Tap "Submit Problem" button to save your problem

4

Generate AI Solution

After saving your problem, you'll be taken to the problem detail screen. Tap "Generate AI Solution" to get personalized advice:

  • The AI analyzes your problem and provides actionable solutions
  • Solutions are concise (max 4 bullet points) and practical
  • You can regenerate solutions if you want different suggestions
Generate AI Solution Button

Tap "Generate AI Solution" button to get AI-powered advice

AI Solution Generated

View your personalized AI solution with actionable bullet points

Free Tier Limit

The free version includes 5 AI solutions per month. Add your own API key in Settings for unlimited solutions!

⭐ Rating Solutions

1

View Your Solution

After the AI generates a solution, scroll down to see the rating section below the solution text.

2

Rate the Solution

Below the AI solution, you'll see a rating section. Tap the stars (1-5) to rate how helpful the solution was:

  • ⭐ 1-2 stars: Not very helpful
  • ⭐⭐⭐ 3 stars: Somewhat helpful
  • ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 stars: Very helpful
  • ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 stars: Extremely helpful
5-Star Rating

Tap the stars below the solution to rate how helpful it was

Track Your Progress

Your ratings help track which solutions work best for you. Check the Analytics screen to see your average rating!

📋 Managing Problems

1

View All Problems

On the main screen, you'll see a list of all your problems sorted by date (newest first).

  • Tap any problem to view details and solutions
  • Use filters to view by category or priority
  • Search for specific problems using the search function
Problem List View

Browse all your problems in the main list view

1.5

Calendar View

Switch to the Calendar tab to see your problems organized by date:

  • View problems on specific dates
  • See your problem-solving timeline
  • Tap any date to view problems from that day
Calendar View

Switch to Calendar tab to see problems organized by date

2

Mark as Resolved

When you've solved a problem, tap the checkmark icon in the top bar of the problem detail screen to mark it as resolved.

Mark as Resolved

Tap the checkmark icon in the problem detail screen to mark as resolved

Track Your Wins

Resolved problems are tracked in your Analytics dashboard - see your success rate!

3

Delete Problems

To delete a problem, open it and tap the delete icon (trash can) in the top bar. This action cannot be undone.

Delete Problem

Tap the delete icon to remove a problem permanently

⚙️ Settings & Customization

1

API Key Settings

Add your own OpenAI API key for unlimited AI solutions:

  • Go to Settings → API Key Settings
  • Add your OpenAI API key
  • Enjoy unlimited AI solutions without rate limits!

Privacy

Your API key is stored securely on your device and never shared with us.

2

Analytics Dashboard

Access your analytics from Settings → Analytics to see:

  • Average solution rating
  • Total problems with solutions
  • Resolved problems count
  • Highly rated solutions (4+ stars)
Analytics Dashboard

View your progress and value metrics in the Analytics screen

3

Demo Mode

Want to replay the onboarding? Enable Demo Mode in Settings to see the onboarding flow again.

💡 Tips & Best Practices

Be Specific

Provide detailed descriptions of your problems. The more context you give, the better the AI solutions will be.

Rate Solutions

Always rate solutions to help the app learn what works best for you and improve future suggestions.

Regular Review

Review your problems regularly and mark them as resolved when solved. This helps track your progress.

Use Voice Input

Voice input is faster and more natural. Try describing problems out loud instead of typing.

Backup Your Data

Regularly export your data to Excel or CSV to keep a backup of all your problems and solutions.

Check Analytics

Visit the Analytics dashboard regularly to see how the app is helping you and track your improvement over time.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many AI solutions do I get for free?

A: The free tier includes 5 AI solutions per month. Add your own OpenAI API key in Settings for unlimited solutions.

Q: Is my data private?

A: Yes! All your data is stored locally on your device. We never access or share your personal problems or solutions.

Q: Can I export my data?

A: Yes! You can export all your problems and solutions to Excel or CSV format from the Export section in Settings.

Q: How do I add my own API key?

A: Go to Settings → API Key Settings → Add New API Key. Enter your OpenAI API key and save it. Your key is stored securely on your device.

Q: Can I use the app offline?

A: You can view and manage your problems offline, but AI solution generation requires an internet connection.
